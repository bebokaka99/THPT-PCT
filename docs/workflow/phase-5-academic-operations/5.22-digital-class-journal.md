# Task 5.22 - Digital Class Journal

> **Phase:** 5
> **Priority:** P1
> **Status:** Hoan thanh v1
> **Dependencies:** 5.6, 5.15, 5.18 va 5.21

## Muc tieu

Tao so dau bai dien tu theo tiet day thuc te: giao vien ghi noi dung tiet hoc,
nhan xet lop, tien do, bai tap va trang thai hoan thanh. Ban ghi phai gan voi
effective timetable slot sau daily override, khong trung mot slot trong cung ngay,
va co audit khi admin chinh ly.

## Pham vi da thuc hien

- Journal entry gan timetable item, lop, mon, hoc ky, ngay day, ca va tiet hieu luc.
- Teacher phu trach tao/sua journal cua minh; substitute teacher la owner sau override
  published.
- Admin xem bao cao, chinh ly khi co permission va bat buoc nhap ly do.
- Hoc ky `closed` hoac `is_locked` chan sua thong thuong.
- Attendance session neu duoc gui len phai dung lop, mon, hoc ky, ngay va tiet.
- Report theo khoang ngay: expected, recorded, missing, completed, draft, cancelled,
  substitute.
- Student/guardian khong co API doc journal de khong lo class comment/progress noi bo.

## Report hoan thanh

- Them migration `046_create_class_journals.sql` voi journal entry, unique
  effective slot/date, immutable audit va trigger kiem tra timetable/semester.
- Them seed `028_class_journal_permissions.sql` voi `class_journals.manage`,
  `class_journals.review`, `class_journals.correct`.
- Backend them `/api/class-journals` cho options theo ngay, list/detail, create/update,
  audit va report.
- Frontend them `/teacher/class-journal` va `/admin/class-journal`.
- Smoke test da phat hien va sua loi tham so trong report khi CTE dung cung from/to
  cho generate_series va day range.
