## Muc tieu

khong cho giao vien, lop hoac phong bi xep trung va chi cong bo lich hop le.

## Pham vi bat buoc

- Cau hinh ca hoc theo truong; mac dinh co ca sang va ca chieu nhung khong hardcode hai ca.
- Cau hinh tiet trong ca voi gio bat dau/ket thuc; mac dinh 5 tiet moi ca.
- Phien ban thoi khoa bieu theo nam hoc/hoc ky voi `draft`, `published`, `archived`.
- Moi tiet lien ket classroom, subject, teaching assignment, teacher va phong hoc.
- Conflict engine kiem tra giao vien, lop va phong tai cung
  `semester + day + shift + period`.
- Admin/teacher xem conflict truoc khi cong bo.
- Giao vien xem dung lich day ca nhan.
- Hoc sinh va guardian chi xem lich published cua lop dang theo hoc.
- Backfill fixture cu va sua du lieu demo trung tiet.

## Ngoai pham vi

- Xep lich tu dong toi uu bang thuat toan.
- Lich theo tuan A/B.
- Day thay va thay doi theo ngay cu the, thuoc Task 5.18.

## Quy tac nghiep vu

1. Mot giao vien khong the day hai lop trong cung slot.
2. Mot lop khong the hoc hai mon trong cung slot.
3. Mot phong khong the duoc hai lop su dung trong cung slot.
4. Assignment phai active va khop lop, mon, hoc ky.
5. Chi timetable khong co conflict moi duoc publish.
6. Thay doi ban published phai tao version moi, khong sua am tham lich cu.
7. API conflict tra ma loi va danh sach slot/doi tuong bi trung.
